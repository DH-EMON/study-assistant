Help

How it works

This is a static website. Everything runs in your browser. There are no accounts and no server, so your data is saved only on this device and browser and is not synchronised to other devices.

  • Materials, Practice, English, Admission: sample content only.
  • Planner, quiz history, bookmarks, timer count: saved in browser storage.
  • Clearing browser data or using private mode can erase your saved items.

Backup and restore

Move your data by hand between browsers or phones.

Privacy

This site does not collect, send or sell your data. It has no accounts, analytics or ads. GitHub, which hosts the site, may keep standard server logs.

Feedback

Report problems or ideas through the Issues tab of this site’s GitHub repository.
