Grammar quick reference

Articles

Use a before a consonant sound (a book), an before a vowel sound (an hour, an honest man), the for something specific.

Simple tenses

Present: She writes. Past: She wrote. Future: She will write. Third-person singular present verbs take -s/-es.

Subject–verb agreement

Singular subject → singular verb (The boy plays). Plural subject → plural verb (The boys play).
