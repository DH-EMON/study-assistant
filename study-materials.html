Newton’s laws and equations of motion · Physics Sample content

1st law: a body stays at rest or in uniform motion unless a net force acts. 2nd law: F = ma. 3rd law: every action has an equal and opposite reaction.
Constant acceleration: v = u + at; s = ut + ½at²; v² = u² + 2as.

Mole concept · Chemistry Sample content

Moles n = mass ÷ molar mass. Particles N = n × 6.022×10²³. One mole of any ideal gas at STP (0 °C, 1 atm) occupies about 22.4 L.

Quadratic equations · Higher Math Sample content

For ax² + bx + c = 0: x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a. Sum of roots = −b/a; product = c/a. Discriminant D = b² − 4ac: D>0 two real roots, D=0 equal roots, D<0 no real roots.

Number systems · ICT Sample content

Binary uses base 2 (digits 0 and 1). Decimal to binary: divide by 2 repeatedly and read remainders upward. 1 byte = 8 bits.

Accounting equation and debit/credit · Accounting Sample content

Assets = Liabilities + Owner’s Equity. Debit increases assets and expenses; credit increases liabilities, equity and income.

Law of demand and elasticity · Economics Sample content

When price rises and other things stay equal, quantity demanded falls. Price elasticity of demand = % change in quantity demanded ÷ % change in price.
